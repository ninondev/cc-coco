Claudie in her non-maid dress (the frames under `plugin/assets/claudie/dake/` and the icons
`plugin/assets/icon/claude-tile-dake-*.png`) follows ZipZipPipe's Claude girl, "Claude 拟人立绘·橘白礼服ver"
(<https://www.bilibili.com/opus/1239937623660691478>). The character design belongs to ZipZipPipe

and not to use them commercially.
