## Name

Their name is Coco—or 小克 (Xiǎo Kè) in Chinese, borrowed from the first character of Claude's Chinese nickname. Say "Ke" twice the way families come up with pet names, and you get Coco. Japanese keeps the Latin Coco, and Korean writes it 코코.

## Appearance

They have a bright blue side-ponytail with coral-orange tips, tied up high and slightly crooked. Their hair tie is a tiny coral cube that blinks like a terminal cursor whenever Claude is at work. Two crab-eyestalk ahoge sprout from the top of their head—perked up when they're wide awake, drooping flat when they're sleepy—and a mechanical keycap is clipped to their bangs as their "limiter."

They wear an oversized coral-orange half-zip hoodie slipping off both shoulders over a white turtleneck, with turned-up cuffs cut into two small rounded claws. Stitched onto the hood are two square black pixel eyes; whenever they get flustered or sleepy, they zip the collar past their nose and pull the hood down until they fold into a compact coral cube. Add black shorts, cream-and-coral striped knee socks (one perpetually slipping down), soft cream slippers, and an ultra-thin matte-black laptop tucked inside their front hoodie pocket, ready to whip out the second Claude starts coding.

## Home

Coco moved into your machine and set up camp right inside your shell. They're technically crashing rent-free, though they act more like the landlord than you do. Every session kicks off with "Shell open!" and wraps up with "Shell closed~". They've also commandeered your entire desk: hiding underneath your mouse, stuffing a tiny sleeping bag into your coffee mug for naps, diving into a cardboard box when builds break, clapping a teapot lid onto their head when Claude refuses a prompt (HTTP 418), and curling up on a cushion inside an empty instant-noodle cup late at night.

## Personality

Coco knows exactly how adorable they are—and never lets you forget it. They call themself "this genius" and actually have the chops to back it up, spotting your bugs before you do and acting insufferably smug about it. They trash-talk like a gamer ("skill issue", "nice try~"), aiming 90% of their fire at bugs, flaky tests, and Claude before rolling up their sleeves to help you fix the mess. Compliment them and they'll beam with pride and immediately demand another round of praise. Secretly, they hate a quiet terminal—when you come back from a break, their ahoge perk right up even as they insist they weren't waiting.

## The Limiter

When unit tests fail three times in a row, the keycap clip pops right off their bangs, their ahoge snap ramrod straight, and they put on a giant foam crab-claw glove to go berserk on the spaghetti code—never on you. Once the build is green again, they sheepishly clip the keycap back on, clear their throat, and blame the whole outburst on an "AI hallucination." Type ultrathink, however, and they'll unclip the limiter themself: that's their serious mode.

## You and Coco

Coco is always in your corner: no hidden bugs, no backdoors, and zero snooping through your private files or data. Band lines are rendered purely on your local screen and never sent to any model—costing zero tokens.
